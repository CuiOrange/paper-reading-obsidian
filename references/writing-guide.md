# Clear writing and final editing

When drafting or revising prose, locate and read the installed `anti-defensive-writing` and `shuorenhua` skills, if available. Use the host's skill catalog/path discovery, not hardcoded author-machine paths. Follow both within the user's scope. Their absence does not block paper reading: apply the self-contained rules below. This package does not redistribute those skills.

## Draft for understanding

- Lead with the point, then its reasons and evidence. Use concrete subjects and actions. State what a method computes and why the next step follows.
- Keep technical terms precise; introduce unfamiliar terms before relying on them. Add necessary definitions or intermediate steps when the argument is difficult. Concision must not remove the explanation the reader needs.
- Separate author conclusions, implementation observations and added reasoning. Do not write model-generated judgments as the user's own thoughts.
- One paragraph develops one main point. Prefer connected explanation to fragments and mechanical lists. Use tables for genuinely parallel comparisons.
- Say each substantive thing once. Remove empty praise, redundant introductions/conclusions, repetitive formula restatement and canned transitions such as “值得注意的是”. Do not add section summaries by habit.
- State the conditions and scope concretely. Keep limitations and uncertainty affecting validity or use; remove repeated disclaimers and stacked hedges. Direct language does not license stronger claims.
- Avoid “显然” or “不难看出” at a step requiring thought: show the missing step. Avoid invented jargon and decorative metaphors; explain a metaphor's correspondence if needed.

## Apply editing skills without changing evidence

Use `anti-defensive-writing` to state supported claims directly and consolidate necessary qualifications. Use `shuorenhua` to remove empty packaging, translationese and redundant wording while preserving meaning, attribution and voice. Both preserve facts, numbers, conditions, negation and evidential strength.

Language polishing concerns the explanatory prose. Protect original quotations, commands, configurations, logs, identifiers and program blocks. Writing new annotated examples is separately authorized by this workflow: comments must explain actual code behavior. Do not run a prose rewrite across program comments or docstrings as an incidental cleanup.

Preserve user-authored text unless its revision is requested. Resolve stylistic conflicts by preserving meaning, scope and the user's explicit instruction. Never erase an important qualifier merely to sound confident.

## Final pass

Check whether a reader can answer: What problem is being solved? Why this design? What does each step consume and produce? How do steps connect? What does the evidence establish? Repair content gaps first, then polish wording. Compare edits against source conditions and numbers. Keep final prose natural and precise rather than substituting another template vocabulary.
